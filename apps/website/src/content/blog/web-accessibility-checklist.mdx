## 1. Keyboard Navigability

Ensure every interactive element is reachable and operable with a keyboard alone:

- **Tab & Shift+Tab:** Can navigate sequentially forward and backward.
- **Escape Key:** Dismisses open modals, dropdowns, and drawers immediately and returns focus to the trigger.
- **Arrow Keys:** Navigates within menus, tablists, and radio groups.

## 2. Perceptible Contrast

- Normal text (< 18pt) requires at least a **4.5:1** contrast ratio against its background.
- Large text (≥ 18pt or 14pt bold) requires at least **3:1**.
- UI components and graphical objects require at least **3:1** against adjacent colors.

With Radian's OKLCH color engine, all semantic tokens (`--fg`, `--fg-secondary`, `--border`, `--primary`) meet these standards across both light and dark themes.

## 3. Semantic Document Outline

- Maintain a single `<h1>` per page.
- Do not skip heading levels (e.g. going directly from `<h2>` to `<h4>`).
- Use landmark regions (`<nav>`, `<main>`, `<aside>`, `<footer>`) to enable quick screen reader landmark jumps.
