## Introduction

Radian is an open-source React component library and UI design system built to accelerate product development with beautiful defaults. Currently, available for **Next.js**, it's powered by **Tailwind CSS** and **Radix Primitives**, providing a solid foundation for modern web apps

<br />
Radian helps you design and iterate quickly, offering components that can serve
as ready-to-use building blocks for your final product or as customizable
starters for unique projects. We've prioritized flexibility, ensuring common
properties and behaviors are baked in—so you won't waste time rebuilding
essentials.
<br />
Our goal? Deliver an exceptional out-of-the-box experience that makes designing,
modifying, and shipping products faster and more enjoyable than ever.

## Advantages of Using Radian

**High Customizability**: Our components look great and are packed with features you can easily change to match your style. The design system is also very versatile in that sense.

<br />

**Open Code System**: All component code lives directly in your codebase, giving you full control to modify and extend without external dependencies after initialization.

<br />
**Rich Design System**: Radian offers one of the most comprehensive and
easy-to-use, free design systems available, continuously improved with designer
feedback.

<br />
**Best Out-of-the-Box Experience**: Zero setup required for polished results.
Our defaults ensure your apps look professional and perform optimally right
away.

<br />
**Growing Blocks Ecosystem**: We're actively developing reusable blocks based on
common patterns and user flows, helping you assemble complex interfaces
effortlessly.

<br />
**AI and beginner-friendly**: With all essential props included, Radian
integrates smoothly with AI code generators. It's also ideal for newcomers,
offering full code access for experimentation and learning.
